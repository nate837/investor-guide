Each company has an auto card with four key numbers from its SEC filings, what each one means, and a simple risk level. Search or filter, then tap a company to open its card.

What the labels mean

  • Size is the company's total stock market value: Mega $200B+, Large $10–200B, Mid $2–10B, Small $300M–$2B, Micro under $300M.
  • Risk level (Lower, Medium, Higher, Very high) is a simple rule of thumb from size, whether it made a profit, debt, share price, and big jumps in sales. It is not a prediction.
  • Sector comes from the industry code the company gives the SEC.
  • In plans: Low and medium plans use only the hand-picked companies with deep research cards. Smaller, riskier names from this list can appear only in High and Extra high plans.
