Trusted Sources

Educational · Not personalized advice

This page is the product’s source policy. Plans cite only the reports below. Prefer primary documents over secondary summaries. No live prices. No market-wide scan.

Allowed sources

  • SEC EDGAR 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K
  • Official company IR earnings releases / transcripts
  • Official ETF fact sheets
  • SEC 13F-HR for the 12 locked firms only (delayed; not a buy list)
  • Berkshire annual shareholder letter
  • Pershing Square official investor letters
  • Third Point official letters
  • ARK official holdings files
  • AQR official research papers
  • Damodaran industry data (context)
  • SEC EDGAR XBRL financial data (the numbers inside filings) — for the company list's key numbers
  • Exchange listing files (Nasdaq Trader) and delayed Nasdaq.com prices — for the company list only, never live quotes
  • Official index-fund holdings files (SPDR SPY / DIA) and Nasdaq's Nasdaq-100 list — only to tag famous-list membership

Banned sources

  • Reddit, TikTok, random blogs
  • Anonymous “smart money” lists
  • Other funds / managers not in the locked 12
  • Guaranteed-return calls
  • Treating a 13F as a live buy list

Why we trust these / why we ban those

Why we trust the allowed list: They are primary filings, issuer documents, or official letters from the locked managers. You can check the original, see the date, and know who published it. That fits a long-term educational project — not a tip sheet.

Why we ban the rest: Social clips, anonymous lists, and “guaranteed” pitches are easy to hype and hard to verify. Other managers are out of scope by design. A 13F is a delayed snapshot (~45 days), not a live buy signal — treating it as one is banned.

Where to look

  • SEC EDGAR — sec.gov/edgar for 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, and 13F-HR. 13Fs are delayed about ~45 days after quarter-end — context only, not a buy list.
  • Company IR — search “[company] investor relations” for official earnings releases and transcripts.
  • ETF fact sheets — issuer sites (Vanguard, iShares / BlackRock, Schwab, etc.) for the official PDF fact sheet for each ETF ticker.
  • Berkshire letter — Berkshire Hathaway annual shareholder letter (company site / annual report).
  • Pershing Square / Third Point / ARK / AQR — official investor letters, holdings files, or research pages from those firms only (see locked 12 below).
  • Damodaran (context) — pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/ (or the current official Damodaran data site) for industry averages — context, not a stock pick.

Reminder: any 13F used for style context is delayed. Never treat it as a live shopping list.
