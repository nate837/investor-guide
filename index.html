What this is

A phone-friendly educational tool for Nate’s college-application project. Answer a short quiz (risk, return, starting amount, time period, sectors), review your choices, then see a sample plan built for your risk level, with each holding in dollars.

No live prices. No market-wide scan. The watchlist shows one delayed end-of-day snapshot, not a live quote. Not personalized financial advice. Markets can lose money. Past performance does not predict future results.
